# Customs Hold Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the rules for handling containers that are under customs investigation at Meridian Port Authority.

The objective of this policy is to ensure that all customs-held containers are processed in a secure, compliant, and traceable manner. It also aims to prevent unauthorized releases, protect public safety, and ensure that every customs decision follows the required legal and operational procedures.

---

## Rules

1. Any container with an active customs hold cannot be released without customs approval.

2. Customs officers must review the reason for the hold before approving any release request.

3. All customs decisions must be recorded for auditing and tracking purposes.

4. A pending release request must remain blocked until the customs hold is removed.

5. Containers involved in investigations require additional verification before any movement.

6. Every customs hold must include a clearly documented reason and the date it was applied.

7. Only authorized customs officers may approve or remove a customs hold.

8. Any attempt to bypass the customs hold process must be logged and reported immediately.

9. Containers with incomplete documentation cannot proceed to the release stage until all missing documents have been provided and verified.

10. If multiple restrictions apply to the same container, all restrictions must be resolved before release approval.

---

## Release Requirements

Before releasing a customs-held container, verify:

- Container ID
- Hold reason
- Hold status
- Customs officer approval
- Release authorization
- Shipping company information
- Carrier authorization
- Supporting customs documents
- Inspection reports
- Any previous customs decisions related to the container

The release process must not continue if any required information is missing or inconsistent.

---

## Inspection Procedure

Before approving a release request, customs officers should perform the following steps:

1. Confirm the container identification number.
2. Review the customs hold reason.
3. Verify the container status in the port management system.
4. Inspect supporting documentation.
5. Review previous inspection records.
6. Determine whether a physical inspection is required.
7. Record the inspection outcome.
8. Approve or reject the request based on the inspection results.

---

## Required Documentation

The following documents should be available before releasing a customs-held container:

- Bill of Lading
- Commercial Invoice
- Packing List
- Customs Declaration
- Import or Export Permit (if applicable)
- Inspection Certificate
- Carrier Authorization
- Dangerous Goods Declaration (for hazardous cargo)

Missing documentation must prevent the release request from being approved.

---

## Risk Classification

Containers may be classified according to operational risk.

### Low Risk

- No customs violations.
- Complete documentation.
- No active investigations.

### Medium Risk

- Additional verification required.
- Minor documentation issues.
- Random customs inspection selected.

### High Risk

- Active customs hold.
- Ongoing customs investigation.
- Hazardous cargo.
- Missing critical documentation.

High-risk containers require additional review before any movement is authorized.

---

## Restricted Case

If a container has:

- Active customs hold.
- Hazardous material flag.

The system must request human approval before continuing the release process.

Additional restrictions may also apply when:

- The container is part of an ongoing legal investigation.
- Customs authorities request manual inspection.
- Required documentation is incomplete.
- Inspection results are inconsistent.
- Security concerns have been reported.

In these situations, automated release processing must stop until manual approval has been completed.

---

## Audit Logging

Every customs action must record:

- Officer ID
- Officer Name
- Timestamp
- Container Number
- Release Request ID
- Decision
- Reason
- Inspection Notes
- Approval Reference Number

Audit records must remain available for compliance reviews, internal investigations, and future reporting.

---

## Exception Handling

The following situations require manual review by senior customs personnel:

- Active customs investigations.
- Hazardous containers under customs hold.
- Missing mandatory documentation.
- Conflicting inspection reports.
- Damaged containers.
- Suspicious cargo declarations.
- Requests submitted outside normal operating procedures.

Release requests must remain suspended until all identified issues have been resolved.

---

## Compliance Requirements

All customs operations must comply with applicable customs regulations and Meridian Port Authority operational procedures.

The customs management system should:

- Prevent unauthorized container releases.
- Maintain complete audit records.
- Track every inspection performed.
- Record every approval and rejection.
- Support compliance and regulatory audits.
- Protect the integrity of customs data.

---

## Record Retention

The customs management system should retain:

- Release requests
- Inspection reports
- Customs decisions
- Audit logs
- Supporting documents
- Officer comments
- Approval history

Records should remain available according to the organization's document retention policy and applicable legal requirements.

---

## Example Scenarios

### Scenario 1

A container has an active customs hold but all required documents are available.

**Result:** The release request remains blocked until customs officers officially remove the customs hold.

### Scenario 2

A hazardous container has both an active customs hold and incomplete documentation.

**Result:** The request is escalated for manual review and cannot proceed until all required approvals and documents have been completed.

### Scenario 3

A container successfully passes inspection and has no active customs restrictions.

**Result:** The customs officer may approve the release after recording the final decision in the audit log.

### Scenario 4

A customs officer discovers conflicting information between inspection records and submitted documents.

**Result:** The release request is suspended until the discrepancy has been investigated and resolved.
